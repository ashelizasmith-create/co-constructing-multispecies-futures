CO-CONSTRUCTING MULTISPECIES FUTURES
Community contribution form — copy and implementation specification

STATUS
Prepared form copy only. No Tally owner account, form, upload destination, or
public form URL is configured by this file. Do not show the confirmation below
in the local preparation prototype. It belongs only to a connected form that
has actually accepted a submission.

Recommended service: Tally, with no respondent login required and a maximum

FORM TITLE
Share an example

INTRODUCTION
What did you make, notice, or try with Co-Constructing Multispecies Futures?
Share an image or a link, a few words about the experience, and the credit you
would like us to use. An unfinished experiment, question, or reflection is welcome.

The project team will review your contribution before deciding whether to display
it in the public examples collection. Sending this form does not publish it.
You do not need a Tally account to contribute.

Your title, contribution, description, context, and chosen credit may be displayed
after review, with your permission below. The optional contact field is private
to the review team and will not appear in the public collection.

FIELDS

1. Title
   Key: title
   Type: short text; required; maximum 140 characters.
   Help: What would you call this example?

2. What are you sharing?
   Key: media_choice
   Type: single choice; required.
   Choices: Image(s) / A media link / Images and a media link
   Implementation: show and require the appropriate fields below so a submission
   always includes at least one image or a complete media URL.

3a. Image(s)
    Key: images
    Type: file upload; required for Image(s) or Images and a media link.
    Accept: JPEG, PNG, WebP. Allow 1–3 images, maximum 10 MB each.
    Help: Include only images you have permission to share. Keep your originals.
    If your photo is HEIC, export a JPEG or PNG copy first. A small, clear image
    is enough; the local preparation tool can help reduce its size.
    Implementation: configure the count/type/size restrictions in the live form
    and check an accepted and a rejected upload before linking it from the site.

3b. Video, audio, or other media URL
    Key: media_url
    Type: URL; required for A media link or Images and a media link.
    Help: Use the complete https:// address of the work. Use a link for video or
    audio rather than uploading a large file. Do not include access tokens,
    private account links, or a participant's personal details.
    Implementation: do not automatically fetch or embed submitted URLs.

4. What did you try or learn?
   Key: description
   Type: long text; required; maximum 5,000 characters.
   Help: Describe the scene or activity, what you noticed as you tried it, and
   what remains open. What surprised you or changed the direction of the work?

5. Where did this happen?
   Key: setting_type
   Type: single choice; required.
   Choices: Independent exploration / A workshop or class / Another gathering
   Follow-up key: workshop_or_gathering
   Type: short text; optional; maximum 180 characters.
   Label: Workshop, class, or gathering name (optional)
   Help: Add a public event name if it helps explain the example. Independent
   exploration is equally welcome. Leave out private participant details.

6. Year (optional)
   Key: year
   Type: short text; optional; four-digit year when supplied.
   Help: When did the activity happen? Leave this blank if you are not sure.

7. Prompts, cards, or tool version (optional)
   Keys: prompts; tool_version_or_deck_edition
   Types: optional long text for prompts; optional short text for the edition.
   Labels: Which prompts or cards did you use? / Tool version or deck edition
   Help: You can paste the prompts from your session slip. If you know the
   Interactive Tool version or the Card Deck filename/edition, include it too.

8. Public credit
   Key: display_credit
   Type: long text; required; maximum 500 characters.
   Help: Write the credit exactly as you want it displayed. Include the maker,
   photographer, and collaborators where relevant. You may use a group name or
   "Anonymous." Do not put your private email or phone number in this field.

9. Describe the image(s)
   Key: image_alt_text
   Type: long text; required when images are uploaded.
   Help: Briefly describe what is visible for someone who cannot see the image.
   For multiple images, use one line each: "Image 1: …", "Image 2: …".
   Include the important action, setting, and materials. A visible person's name
   is not necessary unless it is relevant and approved for public display.
   Implementation: match each description to the correct image during review.

10. Private contact email (optional)
    Key: private_contact_email
    Type: email; optional.
    Help: Add an email only if you would like the project team to contact you
    with questions about this contribution. It will not be displayed in the
    public collection or copied into public files. Leaving it blank is fine.

11. Permission to share the material
    Key: authority_to_share
    Type: required checkbox; unchecked by default.
    Label: I made this material or have permission from its creators and anyone
    identifiable in it to share it with this project and display it as described
    below. The credit I have provided includes the relevant collaborators and
    photographer.

12. Permission to display after review
    Key: permission_to_display
    Type: required checkbox; unchecked by default.
    Label: I give the Co-Constructing Multispecies Futures project permission
    to display this contribution, its description, and the public details I
    supplied on the project's examples pages after review, with the public
    credit above. I understand that submitting does not guarantee publication.

    Supporting text:
    You retain any rights you hold in your contribution. This permission covers
    display with credit and technical resizing or compression for the website.
    It does not transfer ownership, apply CC0 to your contribution, or authorize
    the project to relicense it. Other uses are outside this request. The toolkit's
    existing repository license does not replace this contribution permission.

IMPLEMENTATION AND REVIEW SPECIFICATION

Owner setup
- Use the owner's Tally account. Keep the form URL unconfigured until a real
  form exists and its upload, required-field, and confirmation behavior is tested.
- Keep respondents free of login requirements. Restrict response and uploaded-file
  access to the project review team; do not enable a public responses view.
- Complete the owner's contact/correction route and retention wording before
  opening the form. Do not invent a contact address or a deletion schedule.
- Notifications and exports are private administrative records. Do not send
  messages or connect external automations as part of implementing this draft.

Private intake record
- Keep the original Tally submission ID, received time, submitted fields, upload
  references, exact permission wording/version, checkbox values, and optional
  contact in a private review record outside the public repository.
- Add review status: received / needs clarification / approved / declined /
  withdrawn. Keep reviewer decisions and any correspondence private.
- A submission is never an automatic public-gallery entry. Reviewer approval
  covers the selected image(s), context, title, caption, public credit, alt text,
  display permission, and the absence of private details.

Public source of truth
- Proposed file: site/data/community-contributions.json. Create it when the
  reviewed gallery is implemented; this specification does not create it.
- This one JSON file owns approved community entries. The public page is generated
  from it or reads it; do not maintain a separate, divergent HTML list.
- Suggested public record: schema_version; stable public id; title; description;
  setting_type; workshop_or_gathering; year (or null); prompts (or null);
  tool_version_or_deck_edition (or null); display_credit; images [{path, alt,
  caption, credit, width, height}]; reviewed media_url (or null); rights_statement.
- Add only reviewed public fields. Never copy raw Tally responses or a private
  review record into the public JSON, Git history, page source, browser payload,
  download, or logs. In particular, exclude private_contact_email, provider
  submission IDs, internal notes, permission evidence, and private upload URLs.
- Keep a private mapping between the public id and original submission ID so
  corrections or withdrawal requests can be traced without publishing contact data.
- Preserve each contributor's supplied rights and credit. Display permission
  alone is not permission to include third-party images in a blanket CC0 archive.
  Make the contribution-specific rights scope explicit before publishing assets
  in the repository or including them in a Zenodo release.

Publication and later migration
- For the first implementation, review and promote approved records manually.
  Publish selected optimized files with the public JSON and verify the displayed
  credit and alt text. Do not publish the whole intake queue.
- Keep the public field names and stable public IDs independent of Tally. A later
  form provider or backend can map its intake into the same private review record
  and approved public JSON without changing the gallery's content model.
- Leave the local prototype truthful: it prepares downloads only. A future
  "Send for review" link should point to the configured form rather than replace
  local preparation with a simulated submission-success message.
