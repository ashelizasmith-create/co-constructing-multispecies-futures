# Development notes

## Planned review: anthropomorphism and partial perspectives

**Status: planned review, recorded at the project owner's request.** This note does not claim that all prompts, cards, instructions, or story framing have been rewritten or reviewed.

Review the Interactive Tool's prompt bank, the facilitation guide, and story-framing language for places that impose human emotions, intentions, motives, or social roles on other beings and materials. Distinguish the feelings of human participants and a scene's atmosphere from claims about another being's inner experience.

Keep the manuscript's emphasis on partial, situated perspectives. Attend to species-specific and material capacities, sensory possibilities, timescales, constraints, resistance, and relations. A roleplay or imagined perspective can raise questions and change attention; it does not provide complete knowledge of another being's experience.

During that review:

- Separate observation and relevant knowledge from imaginative invention; leave uncertainty visible.
- Ask whether a prompt lets another being or material resist the role imagined for it.
- Preserve differences between encountering, moving with, and becoming-with; avoid presenting any as full access to another perspective.
- Keep embodied participation adaptable through attention, orientation, gesture, scale, and constraint.
- Record any resulting prompt or guide changes in the changelog. Preserve original source artifacts and earlier numbered tool snapshots.

This is a future editorial and methodological task. The current development update adds orientation, navigation, and citation support; it does not complete this review.
