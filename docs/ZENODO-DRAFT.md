## Proposed title

Co-Constructing Multispecies Futures: Card Deck and Interactive Toolkit for Embodied Worldbuilding

## Proposed description

Co-Constructing Multispecies Futures is a card deck and interactive toolkit for embodied worldbuilding through performance and play. It guides groups through building and telling speculative scenes, drawing on *The Thing From The Future* (Candy & Watson, 2014) and the *Rehearsing Realities* framework (Smith, Leadabrand, et al., in press).

Participants can begin with where and when, adding affect, point of view, form, more-than-human collaborators, pivots, and ways of knowing. They enter a scene through roleplay, movement, and rehearsal, then return to talk, sketch, or write with what the body noticed. Encountering, moving with, and becoming-with offer overlapping approaches to relations with animals, plants, machines, ecosystems, and infrastructures. A rehearsal may lead to further creative work or remain a gesture, question, or unresolved experience.

The toolkit is intended for facilitators, designers, artists, organizers, educators, researchers, and community partners. It supports participation through differences in scale, orientation, attention, and constraint without requiring every body to move in the same way.

The toolkit has two formats: **Card Deck** and **Interactive Tool**, both under the title **Co-Constructing Multispecies Futures**. The planned release includes a 94-card general selection, print-at-home and professional print formats, use guidance, accessible card text, and the Interactive Tool with its adapted prompt bank. A separate browser presents the original cards. Historical editions include the complete 122-card multispecies deck and a 100-card precision livestock farming variant. Provenance identifies each source and adaptation. The archival record will preserve the chosen reviewed release, including a numbered standalone HTML download, with a link to the [current online tool](https://ashelizasmith-create.github.io/co-constructing-multispecies-futures/interactive.html).

The toolkit is discussed in *Performing the What-If: Embodied Worldbuilding with More-than-Human Intelligences, from Roleplay to Emerging Technologies*, a forthcoming chapter by Ash Eliza Smith and Julia McQuillan in *Strategic Handbook of Worldbuilding* (Routledge). The chapter is not yet public and is not included in this deposit. The toolkit's public paper page provides a short overview and publication status.
