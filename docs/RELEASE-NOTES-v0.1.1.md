## What changed and why

This update makes it easier to find, use, and cite the Card Deck and Interactive Tool. Shared navigation leads to Start here, Interactive Tool, Print cards, Facilitate, and About & cite. From the tool, the other pages open in a new tab so a working session remains available.

The About & cite page adds a copyable title-first development citation, a concise orientation to embodied worldbuilding, both primary lineages—*The Thing From The Future* and *Rehearsing Realities*—and the separate reference to the forthcoming *Performing the What-If* chapter. The chapter is not yet public and its manuscript is not included.

A planned review of anthropomorphism is recorded in [DEVELOPMENT-NOTES.md](DEVELOPMENT-NOTES.md): examine imposed human feelings or motives while retaining partial, situated perspectives and species/material capacities, constraints, and resistance. This release does not claim to complete that review or rewrite the full prompt bank.

## Proposed contents

- **Card Deck:** the 94-card general selection, its professional print PDF, Letter/A4 fold-and-glue and duplex editions, separate calibration/instruction sheets, and accessible card data.
- **Historical materials:** the complete 122-card multispecies source and the 100-card PLF variant, distinguished by context and provenance.
- **Interactive Tool:** current source and the numbered 0.1.1 HTML snapshot, with the adapted 84-option prompt bank, draw/hold controls, session exports, and local recording interface. The original supplied HTML and 0.1.0 snapshot remain preserved separately.
- **Website and guidance:** original-card browser, facilitation guide, About & cite, workshop/development history, version history, source manifests, and release documentation.
- **Rights and credits:** the unchanged existing CC0 1.0 dedication and source-grounded narrative credits. Final creator names/order, roles, applicable funding, and licensing scope for added materials remain to be settled in archival metadata.

The unpublished manuscript, full working transcripts, participant records, and private working documents are excluded.
