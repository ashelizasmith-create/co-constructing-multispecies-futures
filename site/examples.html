From Performing the What-If

A vocabulary for trying things.

Encountering, moving with, and becoming-with are overlapping modes of embodied practice. They can be explored through everyday materials as well as emerging technologies.

Read the table as text
Three modes of embodied encounter, from low-fi to emerging technologies. Bleed and attunement move across all three.
ModeCaseWhat the body approachesLow-fiWith emerging technologies

These modes are distinct from the three interwoven phases of Rehearsing Realities: enter the magic circle; prototype with entangled bodies; rehearse transformation. Read about the framework and its lineages.

A photograph, a sketch, an object, a short performance, or a media link can help another group begin. Keep the prompts, a sentence about what happened, and the credit you want shown with the work.

Thinking about facilitating a workshop? Prepare a note about where, with whom, and what you hope to explore. You can be at the idea stage, ask for help, and decide separately whether a future workshop may appear in the public history.
