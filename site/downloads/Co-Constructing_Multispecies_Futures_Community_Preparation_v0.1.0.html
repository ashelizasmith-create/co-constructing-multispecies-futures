What happens to an image?

It is decoded and redrawn locally. Its aspect ratio stays the same, transparency is retained, and the original EXIF metadata is not copied into the prepared image. Quality is reduced toward the 200 kB target; very detailed images may also be reduced further in pixel dimensions. Exact dimensions and size are shown in the preview. A file can remain above the target if further reduction would make it too small.

Choosing JPEG for a transparent image automatically uses WebP, or PNG if WebP export is unavailable. No background is added. Nothing in this page contacts an image service or an upload endpoint.
